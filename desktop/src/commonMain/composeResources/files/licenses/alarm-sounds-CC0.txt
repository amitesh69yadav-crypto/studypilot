Alarm sounds bundled with StudyPilot (desktop: files/sounds/*.wav, Android: res/raw/*.ogg)

All four are from BigSoundBank (https://bigsoundbank.com) and released under CC0 1.0
(public domain): free to use, change and share, no attribution required. They were
resampled to 22.05 kHz mono, trimmed and normalised for use as alarms.

alarm_classic_bell  "Mechanical alarm clock, short ring"  https://bigsoundbank.com/mechanical-alarm-clock-short-ring-s1373.html
alarm_digital       "Electronic Alarm (Buzzer) #3"        https://bigsoundbank.com/electronic-alarm-buzzer-3-s0173.html
alarm_watch_beep    "Digital watch, alarm"                https://bigsoundbank.com/digital-watch-alarm-s2256.html
alarm_buzzer        "Buzzer #4"                           https://bigsoundbank.com/buzzer-4-s1586.html
